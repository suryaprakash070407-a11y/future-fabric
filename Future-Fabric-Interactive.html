Data, scoring & limitations

Water and energy values cover production on the same 1 kg finished-fabric basis. Recyclability is assumed technical potential, not availability of a local recycling service. Lifespan assumes comparable use and care. Garment suitability also depends on fabric weight, weave/knit, blends and finishing.

Seed values are fictional demonstration assumptions, not verified lifecycle assessments. User-provided records are not independently checked. This model does not assess carbon, toxicity, microplastics, social conditions or full lifecycle effects. Do not mix water footprint definitions or inconsistent production boundaries when importing data.

Edits are kept in browser storage when available. File storage varies by browser and file location; use Download updated file in Fabric library for a portable copy with your fabric records, saved comparisons and current selection. Tokens and chat messages are excluded. Browser storage details.

Relative percentage change = (alternative − baseline) ÷ baseline × 100. Recyclability changes use percentage points. A zero baseline has no defined percentage change. Cost per service year is only price divided by lifespan; it excludes care and disposal. Changing your catalogue can change the relative scores.
